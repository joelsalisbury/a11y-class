Challenge 06: Reduce the Friction

Work with your team to review GearShare, a fictional university equipment-loan service.

You need a Camera Kit for a field shoot Thursday afternoon. Use GearShare to find an available kit, arrange pickup, and submit the request.

GearShare contains 12 deliberate issues for you to investigate. Some map directly to accessibility requirements. Others require broader accessibility or inclusive-design judgment.

Use authoritative evidence. Distinguish law, technical standards, institutional requirements, and broader guidance; make only claims your evidence supports. Automated-tool output and AI output are not proof. Do not label findings by module.

Submit one team PDF containing:
• Evaluation of all 12 issues, with Observation, Classification, Evidence, and Recommendation for each
• One redesign artifact addressing at least two concerns
• Top Two priorities
• A brief AI Use Note

10 points. Team submission.

Use the course site for the full challenge instructions and GearShare experience. HuskyCT is authoritative for the submission deadline.
