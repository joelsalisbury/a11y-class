Challenge 07: Prove It

Choose one approved UConn webpage and review it using:
• WAVE
• Chrome Lighthouse
• Manual testing
• One generative AI assistant

Submit one concise individual PDF through HuskyCT, including your findings, evidence, short final reflection, and brief AI Use Note. Screenshots are encouraged where useful. No remediation implementation is required.

10 points. Individual submission.
